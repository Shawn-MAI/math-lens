Imagine standing on a hill and wanting to reach a lower point. The gradient tells you how steep
the hill is and which direction points uphill. Gradient descent moves in the opposite direction,
so a positive gradient moves the weight left and a negative gradient moves it right.

The learning rate controls the size of each move. A small rate takes careful, slow steps. A large
rate can overshoot the low point and even move farther away. Choose a starting weight and learning
rate, then step or play the updates to watch the loss change.
